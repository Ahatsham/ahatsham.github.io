01 / Research focus

Multimodal AI that stays meaningful in the real world.

My work investigates how to make machine learning systems generalize across people, settings, and time. I am especially interested in models that connect qualitative narratives with quantitative trajectories while remaining interpretable and dependable in consequential settings.

  • Clinical AI & maternal safetyPredictive models for hypertensive crises, hemodynamic instability, hemorrhage, and ICU admission using EHR, waveform, genetic, imaging, and text data.
  • Longitudinal language modelingLanguage-model representations for forecasting behavioral, emotional, and learning-related change over time.
  • Reliable multimodal learningCross-distribution evaluation, missing-data robustness, uncertainty-aware modeling, and clinically meaningful AI.

02 / Current work
