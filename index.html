Ahatsham Hayat

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering with a passion for advancing research in artificial intelligence and machine learning. My work focuses on developing large language models to better understand and predict human behavior using longitudinal data.

You can reach me at aahatsham2@huskers.unl.edu or view my work on Google Scholar.

Research Focus

My research centers on large language models and multimodal learning for longitudinal experiential data, with a focus on forecasting engagement, behavior, and confidence over time.
